Showdowns

A Showdown is an alternating window at a contested Battlefield: whoever contested it takes Focus and acts first, Focus moves on each time a Chain closes, and the window shuts only once every player has passed in sequence without playing anything.

This page covers the Window of Opportunity rule 342 calls a Showdown — how it opens at a contested Battlefield, who acts, how Focus moves between players, and what closes it.

The map

The steps

1

Open — the Battlefield is contested

● Grounded

A Showdown opens at a Battlefield whose Control is Contested, during a Cleanup, while the turn is in a Neutral Open State. The player whose units applied that Contested status takes Focus, so the contester acts first. From here the turn is in a Showdown State, and that is a real restriction: by default neither cards nor card abilities can be played at all while one is in progress.

  • CR 344A Showdown begins when Control of a Battlefield is Contested during a Cleanup and the turn is in a Neutral Open State.
  • CR 345As a Showdown begins, the player who applied Contested status to the Battlefield gains Focus.
  • CR 342A Showdown is a Window of Opportunity in which Players have an Open State in which they may play Spells in an alternating fashion.
  • CR 343.1.aCards of all Categories, by default, cannot be played during a Showdown State.
  • CR 343.1.bCard abilities, by default, cannot be played during a Showdown State.

Where you go next

  • 1
    the Showdown has opened and the contester holds Focus Go to step 2 — Act — the player with Focus chooses

    Because CR 345

2

Act — the player with Focus chooses

● Grounded

The player with Focus has exactly two options, and the rules give them as a closed list: play a card or activated ability that is legally timed, or pass. "Legally timed" is doing the work — a Showdown State blocks cards and abilities by default, so what is playable here is generally something with Reaction.

  • CR 347During a Showdown, the player with Focus may do one of the following:
  • CR 347.1Play a Card or Activated Ability that is legally timed.
  • CR 347.2Pass.
  • CR 343.1.aCards of all Categories, by default, cannot be played during a Showdown State.
  • CR 343.1.bCard abilities, by default, cannot be played during a Showdown State.
  • CR 338.1.a.2A Legally Timed Card or Activated Ability would be one with Reaction or one that will have Reaction when played under appropriate circumstances.

    Because CR 347.2.a

3

Resolve — the Chain runs

● Grounded

A spell played into a Showdown is not special: it creates a Chain as normal and is worked through by the ordinary HOT FEPR process. The Showdown does not reconsider Focus until the last item on that Chain has resolved and the turn is back in an Open State.

  • CR 342.1Each spell played this way creates a Chain as normal.
  • CR 346When the last item on the chain resolves and the turn returns to an Open State during a Showdown, Focus passes, and the next Player gains both Focus and Priority.
  • CR 336When there are no outstanding Tasks and there are pending Chain Items on the Chain, players should refer to the FEPR process to proceed.

Where you go next

  • 5
    the Chain closes in the ordinary way — Focus passes to the next player in turn order Go to step 2 — Act — the player with Focus chooses

    Because CR 347.1.b

  • 6
    the Chain opened because a triggered ability or an Add ability went on it — Focus does not pass, and the same player acts again Go to step 2 — Act — the player with Focus chooses

    Because CR 346.1

4

Close — everyone has passed

● Grounded

The Showdown closes only when every player has passed once in sequence without playing a spell or activating an ability. A single pass does not do it; it takes a full round of them.

  • CR 348If all players pass Focus without playing a spell or activating an ability, then the Showdown Closes.
  • CR 347.2.aIf all Players have passed once in sequence, the Showdown ends.

Commonly believed, and wrong

  • Passing during a Showdown ends it.

    Why that's wrongIt ends only if that pass completes a full sequence — every player passing once, in turn, with nothing played in between. Otherwise passing just hands Focus to the next player and the window stays open.

    CR 347.2.a · CR 347.2.b

  • A Showdown is a window in which you can finally play the card you have been holding.

    Why that's wrongThe opposite, by default. A Showdown puts the turn in a Showdown State, and in that state cards and card abilities cannot be played at all unless something makes them legally timed — which in practice means Reaction.

    CR 343.1.a · CR 343.1.b · CR 347.1

  • Whenever your spell resolves, Focus goes to your opponent.

    Why that's wrongNot if the Chain was opened by a triggered ability or by an ability that Adds resources. In that case Focus does not pass, and the player who had it keeps it.

    CR 346.1
