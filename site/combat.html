Combat

Combat is three steps at one Battlefield — a Showdown, then damage, then resolution — and it only begins once units of exactly two opposing players are there; the third step can stage the whole thing again rather than ending it.

This page covers the procedure rules 459-466 call Combat — when it is staged, the three Steps of Combat, how damage is assigned and dealt, and what settles the Battlefield afterwards.

The steps

1

Staged — two players' units meet

● Grounded

Combat is Staged the moment units controlled by two opposing players are at a Battlefield and the Steps of Combat have not started. Staged is not the same as happening: the Combat occurs at a Cleanup, with no items on the Chain and no other Showdown or Combat ongoing anywhere. Exactly two players — never three.

  • CR 461Combat is considered Staged if there are units controlled by two opposing players at a Battlefield but the Steps of Combat have not been initiated.
  • CR 460A Combat occurs when a Cleanup occurs, there are no items on the Chain, there is a staged Combat at a Battlefield, and no Showdown or Combat is ongoing at any other Battlefield. See rule 318. Cleanups for more information.
  • CR 462Combat can only occur between Units controlled by exactly two players.

2

Step 1 — the Combat Showdown

● Grounded

Combat opens with a Showdown, or turns the one already running into a Combat Showdown. Before anyone acts, the designations are fixed: the Attacker is whoever's units applied the Contested status, the Defender is the one who did not, and the Attacker gains Focus. Then play proceeds on the Chain as normal.

  • CR 464.2When Combat opens, it either opens with a Combat Showdown, or the current Showdown becomes a Combat Showdown.
  • CR 464.2.aThe following Tasks become Outstanding, in the order described:
  • CR 464.2.c.1The Attacker is the player whose unit(s) applied the Contested status to the Battlefield.
  • CR 464.2.c.2The Defender is the player who did not apply the Contested status to the Battlefield.
  • CR 464.2.d3. The Attacker gains Focus.
  • CR 464.2.gPlayers proceed with any play on the Chain as normal.

Where you go next

  • 4

3

Step 2 — Combat Damage

● Grounded

Both sides sum the Might of their units, and starting with the Attacker each assigns that total among the other's units. Assignment is not dealing: nothing happens to any unit until every point is assigned, and then all of it lands at once. A unit must be assigned lethal damage in full before the next one is assigned any, and no more than the minimum lethal amount unless nothing else is left to assign to.

  • CR 465.2.aSum the Might of all Attacking Units.
  • CR 465.2.bSum the Might of all Defending Units.
  • CR 465.2.cStarting with the Attacker, each player assigns an amount of damage equal to their summed Might among the other's Units.
  • CR 465.2.c.1Assigning Damage is not Dealing Damage.
  • CR 465.2.c.1.aWhen all Damage is assigned, it will be Dealt simultaneously. These actions are not synonymous.
  • CR 465.2.c.3Units must have lethal damage assigned to them in full before damage is assigned to a different Unit.
  • CR 465.2.c.4Units cannot have more damage assigned to them than the minimum required to constitute lethal damage unless no further units remain to have damage assigned to them.
  • CR 465.2.dDeal Damage to each unit equal to the amount assigned to it.

Where you go next

  • 5
    damage has been dealt — the FEPR process is skipped and any outstanding tasks are cancelled Go to step 4 — Step 3 — Resolution

    Because CR 465.3

4

Step 3 — Resolution

● Grounded

A Combat Cleanup runs first, and it heals every unit and recalls surviving Attackers if Defenders are still there. Only then is the result read: a player won if they are the only one with units left here, lost if they are the only one without. If both still have units, or neither does, there is No Result. Control and any Conquer are settled last, and it need not be the player who contested it.

  • CR 466.11. Perform a Combat Cleanup.
  • CR 466.1.a.1Insert “3c. Heal all Units.”
  • CR 466.1.a.2Insert “3d. Recall Attackers present at the Battlefield if Defenders are still present.” See rule 454. Recalls for more information.
  • CR 466.3.aA Player has won a combat if they received either the attacker or defender designation and are the only Player that has units remaining at this battlefield during this step.
  • CR 466.3.bA Player has lost a combat if they received either the attacker or defender designation and are the only Player that does not have any units remaining at this battlefield during this step.
  • CR 466.3.dThere is “No Result” if units were recalled during step 3d of the Combat Cleanup, if both Players have units present during this task, or if neither player has units present during this task.
  • CR 466.51. If no Showdown or Combat is staged at this location, the player with Units remaining here Establishes Control if they didn’t already control this Battlefield.
  • CR 466.5.dEstablishing Control results in a Conquer if that player has not yet scored this Battlefield this turn. See rule 188. Control for more information on Control. See rule 469.1. for more information on Conquering.
  • CR 466.5.eThis does not have to be the player that applied Contested to the Battlefield.

Commonly believed, and wrong

  • Damage is dealt to each unit as it is assigned, so the first one dies before the next is assigned.

    Why that's wrongAssigning is not dealing. Every point is assigned first, across both sides, and only then is all of it dealt simultaneously — so a unit that will die still has its full Might counted, and abilities that trigger on damage all see the same moment.

    CR 465.2.c.1 · CR 465.2.c.1.a · CR 465.2.d

  • You can dump all your Might onto their biggest unit.

    Why that's wrongOnly up to lethal. A unit must be assigned enough to kill it before any is assigned elsewhere, and no more than that minimum unless there is nothing else left to assign to.

    CR 465.2.c.3 · CR 465.2.c.4

  • Damage marked in combat stays on the surviving units afterwards.

    Why that's wrongThe Combat Cleanup heals all units as part of the Resolution Step, before the result is even read.

    CR 466.1.a.1

  • Surviving the combat means you took the Battlefield.

    Why that's wrongYou win only by being the ONLY player with units left there. If both of you still have units — or neither does — it is No Result, and if both remain the whole thing is staged again.

    CR 466.3.a · CR 466.3.d · CR 466.3.d.1
